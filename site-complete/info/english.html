All Nippon Kyudo Federation

公益財団法人 全日本弓道連盟 (ANKF)

Kyudo is a traditional discipline that uses the Japanese bow for both recreation and sport as well as the training of body and mind.
The All Nippon Kyudo Federation (ANKF) is the governing body for kyudo in Japan and under the auspice of the International Kyudo Federation (IKYF) is responsible for the promotion and development of kyudo worldwide.

The outline of ALL NIPPON KYUDO FEDERATION

All Nippon Kyudo Federation responsibilities:

  1. 1. Establishment of general policies in terms of the dissemination and promotion of kyudo.
  2. 2. Organization of national and international competition and events.
  3. 3. Assessment of instructors (shogo title holders) and examination for the kyudo and dan grade levels.
  4. 4. Implementation of research and studies, and the organization of workshops.
  5. 5. Publication of journals and information.
  6. 6. Other projects necessary to accomplish its purposes.

The birthplace of kyudo is Japan and as such the All Nippon Kyudo Federation is responsible for the development of kyudo and keeping standards of practice to the many national organizations that are found throughout the world.
